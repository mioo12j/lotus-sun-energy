# Civil Works & Fencing

All the civil works a solar plant needs: foundations, cable trenches, inverter rooms, internal roads, drainage, boundary walls and chain-link fencing.

What We Do

## The groundwork behind a good plant

Good civil work keeps a solar plant dry, secure and easy to maintain. Poor drainage, weak trenches or a sagging fence cause problems for years, so we plan the civil scope together with the electrical design.

We can take the civil package on its own for a developer or EPC company, or as part of a complete ground-mount plant.

### What's Included

- Foundations for inverters, transformers, ACDB and control rooms
- Cable trenches, conduits and road crossings
- Earth-pit chambers and covers
- Internal roads and walkways
- Storm-water drainage
- Pre-cast or brick boundary walls
- Chain-link fencing and gates

FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

What civil works does a solar plant need?

Equipment foundations, cable trenches, earth pits, internal roads, drainage and a secure boundary with fencing and gates. Rooftop systems need far less: mainly bases for the structures and inverters.

Can you do only the civil works?

Yes. We can take the civil and fencing package alone for a developer or EPC company.

Which fencing do you recommend?

Chain-link fencing on concrete posts is the usual choice for solar plants; pre-cast walls suit sites that need more security.
