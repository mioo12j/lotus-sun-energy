## India's Five Climate Commitments

Announced by the Prime Minister at COP26 in Glasgow. Every solar system built in India counts towards them.

- 1

India will reach its non-fossil energy capacity to 500 GW by 2030.

- 2

India will meet 50 percent of its energy requirements from renewable energy by 2030.

- 3

India will reduce the total projected carbon emissions by one billion tonnes from now onwards till 2030.

- 4

By 2030, India will reduce the carbon intensity of its economy by less than 45 percent.

- 5

By the year 2070, India will achieve the target of Net Zero.

The five commitments in the Prime Minister's words, from his national statement at COP26, Glasgow, 1 November 2021 ([PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1768712)).

Homes & housing societies

### PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana

Open; apply on pmsuryaghar.gov.in

- Launched on 13 February 2024; approved by the Union Cabinet on 29 February 2024 with an outlay of Rs 75,021 crore
- Central subsidy: Rs 30,000 for 1 kW, Rs 60,000 for 2 kW and Rs 78,000 for 3 kW or more
- Housing societies and RWAs: Rs 18,000 per kW for common facilities, up to 500 kW
- Collateral-free loans of around 7% for home systems up to 3 kW; Indian-made (DCR) modules and a registered vendor required
- Target: 75 lakh households by December 2026, on the way to 1 crore

**Where we fit:** We register you on the national portal, file the DISCOM feasibility and net-metering papers, install a DCR-compliant system and follow the subsidy claim to your bank account.

[Read the full guide](https://lotussunenergy.com/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy/)[Source: PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2268992)

Farmers, cooperatives, panchayats, FPOs

### PM-KUSUM

Closing: complete by 31 Mar 2027

- Component A: grid-connected solar plants of 500 kW to 2 MW on farmers' land, power bought by the DISCOM
- Component B: standalone solar pumps, with 30% central support (50% in the North-East, hill states and islands) plus state support
- Component C: solarisation of grid-connected pumps, individually or by whole feeders
- Financial closure for Components A and C extended to 30 November 2026; completion by 31 March 2027, with no further extension
- PM-KUSUM 2.0, with about 10 GW for agri-photovoltaics, announced but not yet notified

**Where we fit:** Component A and feeder-level plants are ground-mount plants: we can design and build them, including piling, fencing, civil works, installation and commissioning.

[Read the full guide](https://lotussunenergy.com/pm-kusum-scheme/)[Source: Energetica India](https://energetica-india.net/news/mnre-extends-pm-kusum-financial-closure-deadline-to-nov-30)

Businesses that own their plant

### Accelerated depreciation

Available to business owners

- Solar power generating systems are depreciated at 40% a year on the written-down value
- The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from 1 April 2026; the 40% rate for qualifying solar assets is reported to continue
- Half the year's depreciation if the plant is used for less than 180 days in its first year

**Where we fit:** Every plant we build on the CAPEX model belongs to the client, so the depreciation is theirs. Confirm the position with your chartered accountant.

[Read the full guide](https://lotussunenergy.com/accelerated-depreciation-solar/)[Source: Taxguru: Income-tax Act, 2025](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/depreciation-income-tax-act-2025-section-34-rates-provisions.html)

Everyone buying a solar plant

### GST on solar at 5%

In force

- The 56th GST Council cut GST on solar cells, modules and specified devices from 12% to 5%, effective 22 September 2025
- EPC contracts: 70% goods at 5% and 30% services at 18%, about 8.9% overall
- The Andhra Pradesh High Court upheld the 70:30 method for a solar EPC contract in December 2025

**Where we fit:** We set out supply and services clearly in every quotation, so the tax treatment is transparent from the start.

[Read the full guide](https://lotussunenergy.com/gst-on-solar-india/)[Source: pv magazine India, 4 Sep 2025](https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/2025/09/04/gst-on-solar-cells-modules-cut-to-5/)

Grid-connected consumers

### Net metering and time-of-day tariffs

In force; 2026 changes in draft

- Net metering up to 500 kW or the sanctioned load, whichever is lower, under the national consumer rules; states set the details
- Above that, net billing or gross metering
- Time-of-day tariffs make solar hours cheaper and evening peaks dearer
- Draft amendment of March 2026 proposes charges above 5 kW and storage above 500 kW; check whether it has been notified

**Where we fit:** We handle the net-metering application, the DISCOM inspection and, for larger plants, the electrical inspector's (CEIG) approval.

[Read the full guide](https://lotussunenergy.com/net-metering-india/)[Source: Mercom India](https://www.mercomindia.com/government-proposes-amendments-to-electricity-consumer-rights-rules)

Consumers of 100 kW and more

### Green Energy Open Access

In force; state charges vary

- Buy green power from an off-site plant from 100 kW of load (down from 1 MW); captive consumers have no minimum
- Connections in the same electricity division can be aggregated (2023 amendment)
- Single-window applications on the national GOAR portal, with deemed approval if not decided in time
- The ISTS charges waiver ended for projects commissioned after 30 June 2025 and is phasing out in steps

**Where we fit:** Captive and open-access plants are usually ground-mount plants: we can take on the piling, civil works, fencing and installation & commissioning.

[Read the full guide](https://lotussunenergy.com/green-energy-open-access/)[Source: GOAR portal](https://greenopenaccess.in/)

Projects covered by the ALMM order

### ALMM List-I and List-II

In force from 1 June 2026

- List-I sets which solar modules may be used in covered projects
- From 1 June 2026, List-II for solar cells applies: covered projects need List-I modules made with List-II cells
- Exemptions for projects whose bid deadline was before the amendment, and for net-metering and open-access projects commissioned before 1 June 2026
- A List-III for wafers is reported for June 2028

**Where we fit:** We do not manufacture modules, so we specify the ALMM-listed or client-approved makes each project needs and keep the documents.

[Read the full guide](https://lotussunenergy.com/almm-list-ii-solar-cells/)[Source: PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2082901)

Anyone importing equipment

### Customs duty on modules and cells

In force

- Budget 2025-26: BCD on modules cut to 20% with a 20% AIDC (about 40% combined); on cells to 20% with 7.5% AIDC
- Budget 2026-27 left the headline rates unchanged and removed duty on a solar-glass input
- Anti-dumping duty on Chinese-origin cells and modules recommended in September 2025; check the current notification

**Where we fit:** For most home, business and scheme projects the route is now Indian ALMM modules; we compare options and paperwork before you commit.

[Read the full guide](https://lotussunenergy.com/solar-import-duty-india/)[Source: Energetica India](https://www.energetica-india.net/news/union-budget-2025-26-solar-cells-and-modules-duty-cut-to-20-percent)

Every plant: modules, inverters, batteries

### BIS Quality Control Order

In force

- Solar Systems, Devices and Components Goods Order, 2025, notified 27 January 2025
- Modules, solar inverters and storage batteries must meet Indian Standards and carry the BIS mark
- Inverters above 200 kW given extra time on self-certification, reportedly to the end of 2026

**Where we fit:** We check BIS and ALMM documents for every component on site and hand them over with the test reports.

[Read the full guide](https://lotussunenergy.com/solar-quality-standards-bis-cea/)[Source: PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2097219)

Utility tenders and large prosumers

### Storage with solar

Advisory for future tenders

- Power Ministry advisory (Feb 2025): two-hour co-located storage equal to 10% of capacity in future solar tenders
- Viability gap funding for battery storage; a second tranche of about Rs 5,400 crore announced in 2025
- ISTS waiver for co-located BESS commissioned by 30 June 2028

**Where we fit:** We can add batteries where backup matters; for most homes and businesses a grid-connected system sized to daytime use pays back fastest.

[Read the full guide](https://lotussunenergy.com/battery-storage-with-solar/)[Source: pv magazine India](https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/2025/02/20/power-ministry-mandates-co-locating-energy-storage-with-solar-projects/)
