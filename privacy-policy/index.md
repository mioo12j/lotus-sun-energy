# Privacy Policy

What we collect through our forms, why, who processes it, how long we keep it, your rights under India's DPDP Act, and how to reach our Grievance Officer.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

## 1. Who we are

This website is run by **Lotus Sun Energy** ("we", "us"), Pakhanaha Sriram, Muzaffarpur, Bihar 843109, India. We decide how the personal data described here is used, so we are its data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). Contact: [info@lotussunenergy.com](mailto:info@lotussunenergy.com).

## 2. What we collect

We collect personal data only when you choose to contact us. Our contact forms ask for:

- **Name**, **phone number** and **email address** (required), so we can reply;
- **Property type**, **city or town** and **average monthly bill** (optional), so we can understand your needs;
- **Message** (optional): whatever you choose to tell us.

Our [free solar quote](https://lotussunenergy.com/free-solar-quote/) form also asks about your project, so we can prepare a proposal: who the system is for, the services you need, how you would like to pay, the site's state, city, type and area, your electricity bill, tariff, connection type, sanctioned load and backup, when you want to start, whether you own the roof or land, your role, and how and when you prefer to be contacted. It also records which website or campaign link brought you to the form, and an indicative system size and savings estimate worked out in your browser from your bill.

The savings calculator on our home page works entirely in your browser: what you type into it is not sent to us. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever your email contains. We do not run user accounts, advertising or tracking of individuals. Like every website host, our host keeps short-lived technical server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to deliver the site and keep it secure.

## 3. Why we use it

We use your details **only to answer your enquiry**: to reply, arrange a site visit, discuss your project and, if you ask, prepare a quotation. We rely on the consent you give by submitting the form (DPDP Act, section 6). The quote form gives each request a simple label (Hot, Warm or Cold), worked out in your browser from your bill, start timeline, role and roof ownership, so our team can decide whom to call first. It is never used to refuse a quote: every enquiry is answered by a person.

## 4. Who processes it for us

We use these service providers (data processors), each only to provide its service to us:

- **FormSubmit** (formsubmit.co) receives what you type into our forms and forwards it to our mailbox. See the [FormSubmit privacy policy](https://formsubmit.co/privacy.pdf).
- Our **email provider** hosts our mailbox, [info@lotussunenergy.com](mailto:info@lotussunenergy.com), where enquiries are stored.
- **Cloudflare** (Cloudflare Pages) hosts and delivers this website, protects it from attacks and keeps the technical server logs described above. If we switch on Cloudflare Web Analytics, it counts visits in aggregate without cookies and without identifying you. See the [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

If you go ahead with a project, we share the details the scheme or your DISCOM requires (for example your name, address, electricity connection and bank details for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy) with the national portal and your DISCOM, only to process your application. These providers may process data on servers outside India under their own safeguards. We share your data with no one else, except where the law requires it, or where you ask us to (for example with a bank, if you ask us to help with a solar loan).

## 5. Cookies

**Lotus Sun Energy sets no cookies** and uses no advertising, remarketing or tracking of its own. Our fonts, images and scripts are served from our own site, not from third-party networks. Our host, Cloudflare, may set a strictly necessary security cookie (such as __cf_bm) to tell real visitors from bots; it is not used for advertising and expires within hours. The savings calculator stores nothing on your device.

## 6. How long we keep it

We keep an enquiry for as long as we need it to respond and, if a project follows, for the life of that project and its warranties. Enquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted within 24 months of our last contact with you. Records the law requires us to keep longer, such as tax and accounting records, are kept for the period the law sets.

## 7. No sale, no sharing, no marketing lists

**We never sell your personal data, and we never share it for advertising.** Submitting a form **never subscribes you to a newsletter or marketing list**.

## 8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
- correct or complete it;
- delete it;
- stop using it, by withdrawing your consent at any time (this does not affect anything we did before);
- under the DPDP Act, nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated.

Email [info@lotussunenergy.com](mailto:info@lotussunenergy.com) with your request. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India after using our grievance process below.

## 9. Security and children

The site is served only over encrypted HTTPS, and access to our mailbox is restricted to our team. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
