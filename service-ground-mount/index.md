# Ground-Mount Solar

Solar plants on open land for farms, factories, institutions and captive use: designed, built and connected to the grid by one veteran-led team.

What We Do

## Solar on your land

Where the roof is too small or unsuitable, open land makes a superb solar site. Ground-mount plants can face the sun at the ideal angle, are easy to clean and maintain, and can be sized well beyond what a roof allows.

We take the whole job: soil check, layout and design, pile foundations, mounting structures, modules, inverters, cabling, earthing, fencing, approvals and grid connection. Plants on farmland can also fit government schemes such as [PM-KUSUM](https://lotussunenergy.com/pm-kusum-scheme/).

### What's Included

- Site survey, soil check and layout design
- Pile foundations and module mounting structures
- Modules, string or central inverters and cabling
- Earthing, lightning protection and SCADA / monitoring
- Boundary fencing, gates, internal paths and drainage
- Module cleaning system with water points
- DISCOM / open-access approvals and grid synchronisation

FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

How much land does a ground-mount plant need?

As a guide, about 4 to 5 acres per MW with fixed-tilt structures, depending on the module type, row spacing and the shape of the land.

Can a ground-mount plant power a factory or farm away from the land?

Yes, under open-access or captive rules in many states, or as a scheme plant feeding the DISCOM. We explain what your state allows before you invest.

Do you handle fencing and civil works too?

Yes: piling, cable trenches, inverter and control-room foundations, internal roads, drainage, boundary walls and chain-link fencing.
