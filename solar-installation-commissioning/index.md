# Solar Installation & Commissioning

Veteran-led installation crews for homeowners, businesses, EPC companies and developers: structures, modules, cabling, earthing, testing and grid synchronisation, done right first time.

What We Do

## Built to the design, tested before switch-on

Installation and commissioning (I&C) is everything that happens on site once the design and equipment are fixed. It decides whether a system performs safely for 25 years, so we treat it with the same discipline the armed forces taught us: a clear plan, checks at every stage and nothing left loose.

We work for owners who have bought their own equipment, and as installation partner to EPC companies and developers, building to your drawings and approved makes. See our [commissioning checklist](https://lotussunenergy.com/solar-plant-commissioning-checklist/) for the steps.

### What's Included

- Equipment foundations and cable trenches
- Mounting structure erection and alignment
- Module installation and string wiring with proper MC4 crimping
- Inverters, ACDB/DCDB and AC cabling
- Earthing, lightning arrestors and surge protection
- Insulation, polarity and earth-resistance tests
- Grid synchronisation, commissioning and handover

## Frequently Asked Questions

What does solar installation and commissioning include?

Mounting structures, module installation, DC and AC cabling, inverters and distribution boards, earthing and lightning protection, testing, grid synchronisation and commissioning.

Can you install equipment I have already bought?

Yes, after checking that it is certified and suited to the site. We record serial numbers and documents before installation.

Do you work as installation partner for EPC companies?

Yes. We can build to your drawings and approved makes, with daily progress reporting and a complete handover file.
