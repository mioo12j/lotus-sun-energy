Home/Services/EPC Turnkey
What We Do

Single-window solar delivery

With turnkey EPC you deal with one company for everything: we engineer the system, procure every component, build it, get it approved and commission it. No juggling separate vendors for panels, inverters, structures, wiring and paperwork.

Every quotation comes with a bill of quantities naming the makes, a generation estimate and payment milestones tied to progress, so you know exactly what you are buying.

What's Included

  • Engineering: layout, structure, electrical design and yield estimate
  • Procurement: modules, inverters, structures, cables, protection and monitoring
  • Construction: civil works, structures, installation and earthing
  • Approvals: DISCOM, net metering, electrical inspector (CEIG) where required
  • Testing, commissioning and handover documents
  • Training and an O&M plan

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does EPC mean in solar?
Engineering, procurement and construction: a single contract that covers designing the system, buying all the equipment and building and commissioning it.
Which brands do you use?
We do not manufacture equipment. We specify BIS-certified, ALMM-listed modules and reliable inverters suited to your site, and name the makes in the quote.
Who handles the approvals?
We do: DISCOM feasibility, net metering and, for larger plants, the electrical inspector's approval.
