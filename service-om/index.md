# O&M & Support

Operation and maintenance that keeps your solar system at peak output: cleaning, inspections, inverter checks, fault response and generation reports.

What We Do

## Keep every panel earning

Dust, bird droppings, loose connections and a tripped inverter can quietly eat into your savings. A simple maintenance plan catches these early, so the system keeps producing what it should for 25 years.

We offer maintenance for systems we install and for existing systems installed by others, from a yearly check-up for a home to a full O&M contract for a commercial or ground-mount plant. Read our [O&M and module cleaning guide](https://lotussunenergy.com/solar-om-module-cleaning/).

### What's Included

- Scheduled module cleaning or cleaning-team training
- Visual and thermal inspection of modules and connections
- Inverter health checks and firmware updates
- Earthing and lightning-protection tests
- Monitoring of generation against expectation
- Fault response and warranty claims
- Monthly or yearly generation reports

FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

How often should solar panels be cleaned?

Usually every 2 to 4 weeks, more often in dusty, dry months and less in the monsoon. Use plain water and a soft brush or mop, early in the morning.

Do you maintain systems installed by other companies?

Yes. We start with an inspection and a report on the system's condition, then suggest a plan.

How do I know my system is performing well?

Compare its monthly generation with the expected figure for your size and location. We set up monitoring and flag any drop.
