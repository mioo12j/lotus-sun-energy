Veteran-Led

From Olive Green to Go Green

Lotus Sun Energy is led by ex-servicemen. Having served the nation in uniform, we now serve it by bringing clean, affordable solar power to India's homes and businesses.

Home/About/Veteran-Led
A second innings of national serviceThe armed forces teach a way of working: plan carefully, do the job right, look after your people and keep your word. Those habits are exactly what a good solar installation needs.

"Olive green" is the colour of the Indian Army uniform. "Go green" is the mission of clean energy. Our motto joins the two: the men and women who served the nation in uniform can keep serving it by helping India meet its clean-energy goals, one rooftop and one plant at a time.

India has committed to 500 GW of non-fossil power capacity by 2030 and to net zero by 2070 (see India's climate commitments). Rooftop and small solar plants on millions of homes, farms and businesses are a big part of that. We want to help build them properly.
