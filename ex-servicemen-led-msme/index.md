# From Olive Green to Go Green

Lotus Sun Energy is led by ex-servicemen. Having served the nation in uniform, we now serve it by bringing clean, affordable solar power to India's homes and businesses.

**A second innings of national service**The armed forces teach a way of working: plan carefully, do the job right, look after your people and keep your word. Those habits are exactly what a good solar installation needs.

"Olive green" is the colour of the Indian Army uniform. "Go green" is the mission of clean energy. Our motto joins the two: the men and women who served the nation in uniform can keep serving it by helping India meet its clean-energy goals, one rooftop and one plant at a time.

India has committed to 500 GW of non-fossil power capacity by 2030 and to net zero by 2070 (see [India's climate commitments](https://lotussunenergy.com/solar-schemes-india/#panchamrit)). Rooftop and small solar plants on millions of homes, farms and businesses are a big part of that. We want to help build them properly.

### Discipline

A written plan for every job, a checklist at every stage and a site left clean and safe.

2

### Integrity

Honest advice, transparent pricing and no over-selling, even when a smaller system means a smaller bill for us.

3

### Safety

Proper earthing, protection and safe working at height. Nobody gets hurt and nothing is left unsafe.

One team responsible for the result, from the first visit to support years later.

5

### Timeliness

Dates we set, we keep, and if something outside our control slips, you hear it from us first.

6

### Service

Looking after the people we work for, long after the job is done.
