Local, Not Far Away

Solar from a team that lives here

Lotus Sun Energy is based at Pakhanaha Sriram in Muzaffarpur. We know Bihar's homes, roofs, weather and DISCOM processes, and we are close enough to visit your site, install carefully and come back whenever you need us.

Bihar is well suited to solar: most of the state receives roughly 4.5 to 5 kWh of sunlight per square metre per day, so 1 kW of panels typically makes about 4 units a day averaged over the year. With domestic tariffs rising and power cuts still common in many areas, a rooftop system (with batteries if you want backup) pays back quickly.

Who looks after what in Bihar

  • NBPDCL (North Bihar Power Distribution Company) serves north Bihar, including Muzaffarpur, Vaishali, Darbhanga, Samastipur, Sitamarhi and the Champarans
  • SBPDCL (South Bihar Power Distribution Company) serves south Bihar, including Patna, Gaya, Bhagalpur, Nalanda and Bhojpur
  • Your DISCOM checks feasibility, installs the net meter and inspects the system; we prepare and file the paperwork
  • Home subsidy applications go through the national portal, pmsuryaghar.gov.in, and the subsidy is paid to your bank account
  • BREDA (Bihar Renewable Energy Development Agency) is the state's nodal agency for renewable energy

Designed for Bihar's weather

  • Raised structures and good drainage for heavy monsoon rain and waterlogged areas
  • Wind-rated mounting for pre-monsoon storms
  • Proper earthing and lightning protection: the state sees frequent lightning
  • Cleaning plans for the dusty months before the monsoon
  • Hybrid inverters with batteries where power cuts are frequent

FAQ

Solar in Bihar: Common Questions

Does the PM Surya Ghar subsidy apply in Bihar?
Yes. PM Surya Ghar is a central scheme available across India, including Bihar: ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and ₹78,000 for 3 kW or more for a home. Ask us whether any state top-up is available when you apply; we will tell you the current position.
Which DISCOM handles net metering in Muzaffarpur?
Muzaffarpur is served by NBPDCL (North Bihar Power Distribution Company). We file the feasibility and net-metering application with NBPDCL, or with SBPDCL if you are in south Bihar.
Is solar worth it in Bihar with the monsoon?
Yes. Output dips on cloudy monsoon days, but the sunny months more than make up for it, and net metering lets you bank summer surplus against the monsoon. Over a year, 1 kW typically produces around 1,400 to 1,500 units.
Can I get backup during power cuts?
A standard on-grid system shuts down during a grid outage for safety. For backup, choose a hybrid system with batteries; we will show you the difference in cost honestly.
