# Solar for Every Kind of Site

Homes, housing societies, shops, offices, schools, hospitals, factories and farms: what solar does for each, and how we design for it.

Rooftop systems of 1 to 10 kW that cut the family bill by up to 90%, with up to ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy and easy EMIs.
