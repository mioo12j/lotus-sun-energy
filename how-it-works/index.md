The Basics

## How Solar Makes Power

1

### Panels capture sunlight

Solar cells turn sunlight into DC electricity. In most of India, 1 kW of panels makes about 4 units a day on average.

2

### The inverter converts it

The inverter turns DC into the AC power your fans, lights, fridge and machines use, and matches it to the grid.

3

### You use it first

Solar power feeds your own loads first, so you buy fewer units from the DISCOM during the day.

4

### Surplus goes to the grid

With net metering, extra units are exported and credited against what you draw at night or on cloudy days.

Working With Us

## From First Call to Switch-On

For a typical home: about 2 to 4 days of work on site, and a few weeks in all, depending on your DISCOM.

- 1

**Enquiry**

Send us a message or fill in the free quote form with your location and a recent bill. We call you back within one working day.

- 2

**Free site visit**

We check the roof or land, shade, structure, wiring and sanctioned load, and answer all your questions.

- 3

**Design and quotation**

A system sized to your use, a generation and savings estimate, named makes, and one all-inclusive price with GST and subsidy shown.

- 4

**Registration and feasibility**

For homes we register you on the PM Surya Ghar portal; for every grid-connected system we apply to the DISCOM for feasibility and net metering.

- 5

**Installation**

Structure, panels, inverter, earthing, lightning protection and wiring, installed neatly and safely.

- 6

**Net meter, inspection and commissioning**

The DISCOM fits the bi-directional meter and inspects the system; we commission it and hand over the documents.

- 7

**Subsidy credited**

For homes, we follow up the subsidy claim until it reaches your bank account.

- 8

**Support**

Monitoring, cleaning advice, yearly check-ups and help whenever you need it.
