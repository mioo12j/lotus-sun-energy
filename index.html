How Solar Works

Your Roof Turns Sunlight Into Savings

Panels on your roof make electricity from sunlight; the inverter makes it usable for your fans, lights and appliances; and any extra power goes to the grid through net metering, earning you credit on your bill.

1

Panels

Solar panels on your roof turn sunlight into clean DC electricity.

2

Inverter

The inverter converts it to AC power for your fans, lights, fridge and appliances.

3

Net Meter

Surplus power goes to the grid and is credited against the units you draw at night.

4

You Save

Lower bills from the first month, and largely free power for 25 years and more.

FAQ

Your Solar Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know before going solar with Lotus Sun Energy.

How much does rooftop solar cost for a home?
A typical home system costs about ₹55,000 to ₹65,000 per kW before subsidy. After the PM Surya Ghar subsidy (up to ₹78,000), most homes pay back the cost in roughly 3 to 5 years and then enjoy largely free power for 25 years and more. Try our savings calculator for your numbers.
What government subsidy can I get?
Under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, homes get ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW, up to ₹78,000. Housing societies get ₹18,000 per kW for common facilities. We handle the whole application for you. Read the subsidy guide.
Is my roof suitable for solar?
Most are. You generally need about 80 to 100 sq ft of shade-free roof per kW. During our free site visit we check the roof area, direction, shade and your usage, then recommend the right size.
Can I pay in EMIs?
Yes. Banks offer solar loans, and home systems up to 3 kW can get collateral-free loans at around 7% under the scheme. In many cases your EMI is close to your current bill, so you save from day one.
How long does installation take?
Once the DISCOM feasibility is approved, the physical installation usually takes just 2 to 4 days for a home rooftop.
What about maintenance and the monsoon?
Solar needs very little maintenance: rinse the panels every few weeks and get a yearly check-up. Panels still generate on cloudy days (at lower output), and net metering lets you bank surplus summer power against monsoon months.
Do you also do commercial and ground-mount solar?
Yes. Besides homes, we design and build rooftop systems for shops, offices, schools and factories, and ground-mount plants on open land, including piling, civil works, fencing and maintenance.
Why choose a veteran-led solar company?
Lotus Sun Energy is led by ex-servicemen. That means discipline, careful planning, strict safety and keeping our word on every site, with honest advice instead of pushy sales.
