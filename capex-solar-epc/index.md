What We Do

## What the CAPEX model means for you

Under the CAPEX (capital expenditure) model you buy the solar system outright. There is no long-term power purchase agreement and no developer margin on every unit: the savings, the subsidy (for homes) and the depreciation (for businesses) are all yours.

For most homes and businesses this gives the lowest lifetime cost of power. If paying upfront is a stretch, a solar loan or EMI spreads the cost, often for about what you pay the DISCOM today. Read our [CAPEX vs OPEX guide](https://lotussunenergy.com/capex-vs-opex-solar/) for the full comparison.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What is the CAPEX model for solar?

You pay for the system and own it from day one. We design, supply, install and commission it; every unit it generates is yours, with no long-term power purchase agreement.

Is CAPEX better than OPEX?

Usually yes over 25 years, because you do not pay a developer's margin on every unit. OPEX avoids the upfront cost but locks you into a long contract. We will compare both honestly for your site.

Can I still pay monthly?

Yes. A bank solar loan or EMI lets you own the system while spreading the cost; for homes up to 3 kW, the scheme offers collateral-free loans at around 7%.
