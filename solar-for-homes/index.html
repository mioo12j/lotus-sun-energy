What We Do

Turn your roof into a power plant

Your roof sits in the sun all day. Rooftop solar puts that free sunlight to work: it generates clean electricity for your home, cuts your monthly bill and protects you from ever-rising tariffs. With the PM Surya Ghar subsidy and easy EMIs, it is more affordable than most families expect.

No running around government offices or chasing vendors. From your first question to long-term service, Lotus Sun Energy takes care of it all, so you can simply enjoy the savings.

What's Included

  • Free site visit, shade check and an honest recommendation
  • System sized to your bill and sanctioned load
  • Indian-made (DCR) modules where the subsidy needs them, with warranties in writing
  • Wind-rated structure suited to your RCC or sheet roof, with leak-proof fixings
  • On-grid inverter with app monitoring (hybrid with batteries if you want backup)
  • PM Surya Ghar portal, DISCOM feasibility, net meter, inspection and subsidy claim handled
  • Neat, safe installation, earthing and lightning protection
  • Help with EMI and solar-loan paperwork

Which size fits your home?

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What size solar system does my home need?
As a guide: bills of ₹1,000 to ₹2,500 a month suit 1 to 2 kW, ₹2,500 to ₹4,500 suit about 3 kW (which also gets the full ₹78,000 subsidy), and bills above ₹5,000 need 5 kW or more. We confirm the size from your bills and roof on the free site visit.
How much subsidy will I get?
₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and ₹78,000 for 3 kW or more under PM Surya Ghar. The subsidy is credited to your bank account after the system is installed and inspected.
Will I still have power during a power cut?
A standard on-grid system switches off during a grid outage for safety. If you want backup, we can fit a hybrid inverter with batteries; we will show you the extra cost honestly.
Can I pay in EMIs?
Yes. Banks offer solar loans, and home systems up to 3 kW can get collateral-free loans at around 7% under the scheme. The EMI is often close to the bill it replaces.
