### Which size fits your home?

**1–2 kW**Smaller homes

Bills around ₹1,000 to ₹2,500 a month. Needs roughly 100 to 200 sq ft of clear roof.

**3 kW**Most family homes

Bills around ₹2,500 to ₹4,500 a month. The most popular size, and it gets the full ₹78,000 subsidy.

**5 kW+**Large homes

Bills above ₹5,000 a month, or homes with ACs and pumps. Sized to your use for bigger savings.

FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

What size solar system does my home need?

As a guide: bills of ₹1,000 to ₹2,500 a month suit 1 to 2 kW, ₹2,500 to ₹4,500 suit about 3 kW (which also gets the full ₹78,000 subsidy), and bills above ₹5,000 need 5 kW or more. We confirm the size from your bills and roof on the free site visit.

How much subsidy will I get?

₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and ₹78,000 for 3 kW or more under PM Surya Ghar. The subsidy is credited to your bank account after the system is installed and inspected.

Will I still have power during a power cut?

A standard on-grid system switches off during a grid outage for safety. If you want backup, we can fit a hybrid inverter with batteries; we will show you the extra cost honestly.

Can I pay in EMIs?

Yes. Banks offer solar loans, and home systems up to 3 kW can get collateral-free loans at around 7% under the scheme. The EMI is often close to the bill it replaces.
