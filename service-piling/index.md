# Pile Foundation & Piling

Solar pile foundations designed for your soil: so ground-mount structures stay level and firm through monsoon floods and storms for 25 years and more.

What We Do

## Foundations that last the life of the plant

A ground-mount plant is only as good as its foundations. The right pile depends on the soil, the water table and the wind loads of your area, so we start with a soil check and design the piles to suit, rather than using one size for every site.

We set out the rows accurately, auger or drive the piles, cast them with proper concrete and curing, and test a sample for pull-out strength before the structures go up.

### What's Included

- Soil investigation and pile design
- Survey and setting-out of rows
- Augered cast-in-situ piles or driven steel piles
- Concrete mix control and cube tests
- Pile-cap and foundation bolt alignment
- Pull-out and lateral load tests
- Records for every pile

FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

Why does a solar plant need pile foundations?

Piles anchor the mounting structures so they resist wind uplift and stay level on soft or flood-prone ground for the life of the plant.

Augered or driven piles: which is better?

It depends on the soil. Augered cast-in-situ piles suit most Indian soils; driven steel piles are faster where the soil allows. The soil test decides.

Do you test the piles?

Yes: concrete cube tests and pull-out and lateral load tests on sample piles, with the results handed over.
