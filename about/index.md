About Us

# Clean Energy, Made Simple

Lotus Sun Energy is a veteran-led, customer-first solar company with one clear purpose: to help every home and business in India enjoy clean, affordable power and freedom from rising electricity bills.

Serving the Nation, **From Olive Green to Go Green**.

Muzaffarpur, Bihar

Our Story

## Why We Started Lotus Sun Energy

We started Lotus Sun Energy because going solar should be easy, honest and within reach of ordinary families and small businesses, not confusing, overpriced or full of fine print. Too many people want to switch to solar but get lost in jargon, paperwork and pushy sales. We are here to change that.

Our name says it all. The **lotus** rises clean and beautiful from the water; the **sun** gives endless, free energy. Together they reflect what we believe in: bringing the clean power of the sun to every rooftop, simply and sustainably.

We are led by ex-servicemen. Having served the nation in olive green, we now serve it by helping India go green, with the same discipline, integrity and sense of duty.

Mission & Vision

## What We Work Towards

### Our Mission

Make solar effortless and affordable: put clean, money-saving solar within reach of every home and business in India, by handling the hard parts ourselves, offering honest pricing and standing behind our work for the long run.

### Our Vision

A sunlit, self-reliant India, where homes, shops, farms and factories across the country generate their own clean power: lower bills, cleaner air and energy independence for every family.
