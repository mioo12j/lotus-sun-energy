Solar Glossary

Solar Terms, in Plain English

The words you will meet when going solar, from kW and net metering to DISCOM, ALMM and payback.

Home/Solar Guides/Solar Glossary
ABCDEGHIKLMNOPRST

A

Accelerated depreciation
A tax benefit that lets a business that owns a solar plant write off its cost faster than normal assets, reducing taxable income in the early years.
ACDB / DCDB
AC and DC distribution boards: the enclosures that house the fuses, isolators and surge protection between the panels, the inverter and the grid connection.
ALMM
Approved List of Models and Manufacturers: the list of solar modules (and, from June 2026, cells) approved by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) for covered projects.

B

Balance of system (BOS)
Everything in a solar system except the panels: mounting structures, inverters, cables, distribution boards, earthing, lightning protection and monitoring.
Bifacial module
A solar module that generates power from both sides, using light reflected from the roof or ground onto its rear face.

C

CAPEX model
You pay for the solar system upfront (or with a loan) and own it outright, keeping all the savings.
Commissioning
The final stage of an installation: testing every component, synchronising the system with the grid and handing it over ready to generate.
CUF (capacity utilisation factor)
The energy a system actually generates in a year as a percentage of what it would generate running at full capacity all year.

D

DCR (domestic content requirement)
A rule in some government schemes, including PM Surya Ghar, that modules and cells must be made in India.
DISCOM
Your electricity distribution company, which approves net metering, installs the bi-directional meter and inspects grid-connected systems.

E

Earthing
Connecting the system's metal structures and electrical equipment to the ground so that fault currents and lightning are carried safely away.
EMI
Equated monthly instalment: paying for a solar system in fixed monthly amounts through a loan instead of all at once.
EPC
Engineering, procurement and construction: a single contract covering the design, equipment purchase and building of the whole system.

G

Grid-connected (on-grid) system
A solar system connected to the utility grid, which can draw from or export to the grid; the most common type for homes and businesses.
Ground-mount solar
Panels installed on structures fixed to the ground, typically on pile foundations; used for farms, factories and open land.

H

Hybrid system
A solar system with batteries that also connects to the grid, so it can keep essential loads running during power cuts.

I

I&C (installation & commissioning)
The on-site work of building a solar system to an agreed design and bringing it into operation: structures, panels, cabling, inverters, earthing, testing and commissioning.
Inverter
The equipment that converts the direct current (DC) from the panels into alternating current (AC) for use at home or export to the grid.

K

kW / kWp
Kilowatt and kilowatt-peak: the rated output of a solar system under standard test conditions. A 3 kW home system has about 3 kWp of panels.
kWh (unit)
A kilowatt-hour, the 'unit' on your electricity bill. In most of India, 1 kW of panels makes about 4 units a day on average.

L

Lightning arrestor
A device that intercepts lightning strikes and conducts them safely to earth, protecting panels and equipment.

M

MMS (module mounting structure)
The steel or aluminium frame that holds the panels at the designed tilt and height.

N

National Portal
pmsuryaghar.gov.in, the website where households apply for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy and choose a registered vendor.
Net metering
A billing arrangement where the power your solar system exports to the grid is offset against the power you draw from it.

O

Off-grid system
A solar system with batteries that is not connected to the grid; used where there is no reliable grid supply.
OPEX / RESCO model
A developer finances and owns the plant on your roof or land, and you buy its power at an agreed tariff for many years.

P

Payback period
The time it takes for the savings on your electricity bill to equal what you paid for the solar system.
Performance ratio (PR)
Actual energy generated divided by the theoretical energy available from the sunlight; a measure of how well a system is built and maintained.
Pile foundation
A steel or concrete pile driven or cast into the ground to anchor the mounting structures of a ground-mount plant.
PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana
The central government scheme that subsidises rooftop solar for homes: up to Rs 78,000 for a system of 3 kW or more.

R

Rooftop solar
Panels installed on a building's RCC or metal-sheet roof, turning unused roof space into a power source.

S

Sanctioned load
The maximum load (in kW or kVA) your DISCOM has approved for your connection; it usually caps the size of a net-metered system.
Shadow analysis
A check of where trees, water tanks, parapets and nearby buildings cast shade on the roof through the day and year, so panels go where the sun is.

T

Tier-1 module
A module from a large, bankable manufacturer with an established production and financing track record.
TOPCon
Tunnel oxide passivated contact: a high-efficiency solar cell technology now common in new modules.
