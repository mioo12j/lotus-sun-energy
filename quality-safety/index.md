Quality & Safety

# Built Right, Built Safe

How we make sure every solar system we build is safe, compliant and ready to perform for 25 years: certified equipment, Indian standards, careful workmanship and full testing.

Our Standards

## What we check on every job

Solar is electrical work on roofs and in the open, designed to run unattended for decades. Quality and safety are not extras: they are the job. These are the standards we build to. For the detail behind them, read our guide to [solar standards in India](https://lotussunenergy.com/solar-quality-standards-bis-cea/).

### Certified equipment

- Modules on MNRE's ALMM list and carrying the BIS mark, as the Quality Control Order requires
- Indian-made (DCR) modules where the PM Surya Ghar subsidy or a scheme needs them
- BIS-certified inverters with the protection functions your DISCOM requires
- Make, model and serial numbers recorded and matched to the documents on delivery

### Sound design

- Structure designed for your area's wind speed and your roof's strength
- Shade analysis so every panel gets the sun
- Cables sized for low losses, with DC and AC protection in proper distribution boards
- Earthing and lightning protection to the relevant Indian standards and CEA safety regulations

### Safe installation

- Safe working at height: harnesses, ladders and edge protection
- Neat cable routing in conduit or trays, properly crimped MC4 connectors
- Isolation and lock-out before any electrical work
- Site cleaned and left safe at the end of every day

### Testing and handover

- Insulation resistance, polarity, open-circuit voltage and earth-resistance tests
- Inverter settings checked and grid synchronisation verified
- DISCOM inspection and, for larger plants, the electrical inspector's (CEIG) approval
- Handover file: test reports, warranties, approvals, single-line diagram and care guide

## Standards We Follow

Modules: **ALMM List-I, BIS mark**

Inverters: **BIS certified**

Safety: **CEA regulations**

Subsidy systems: **DCR modules**
