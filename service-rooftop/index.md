# Rooftop Solar

On-grid rooftop solar for homes, housing societies, shops, offices, schools and factories, turning unused roof space into a clean, bill-cutting power source.

What We Do

## Rooftop solar, done properly

From RCC terraces to large metal-sheet sheds, we design rooftop systems that make the most of your roof while protecting it. We check the structure and the shade, choose the right mounting, liaise with your DISCOM for net metering and commission the system safely.

A rooftop system uses space you already own, so there is no land, fencing or piling cost, and the power is used right where it is made. For homes the [PM Surya Ghar subsidy](https://lotussunenergy.com/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy/) applies; for businesses, [accelerated depreciation](https://lotussunenergy.com/accelerated-depreciation-solar/) and [5% GST](https://lotussunenergy.com/gst-on-solar-india/) help the numbers.

### What's Included

- Structural and shadow analysis
- Leak-proof mounting for RCC and metal-sheet roofs
- Elevated structures where you want to use the terrace below
- Net-metering and DISCOM liaison
- BIS-certified modules and on-grid or hybrid inverters
- Walkways, earthing and lightning protection
- Generation monitoring on your phone

FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

Will rooftop solar damage my roof?

No. We use structurally designed mounting with waterproofed fixings on RCC roofs and sealed clamps on metal sheets, after checking the roof's condition and load.

What is net metering and do you handle it?

Surplus solar power is exported to the grid and credited against the units you draw. We handle the DISCOM application, the bi-directional meter and the inspection.

What size rooftop system can you install?

From a 1 kW home system to large rooftops on factory sheds. Size is limited by your shade-free roof area and your sanctioned load.
